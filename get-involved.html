Whether you are a parent seeking support for your child, an educator or therapist who wants Venturable's services at your site, or a student who wants to make a difference in their community. Venturable makes it easy to get involved.

Join us

Three ways people come to Venturable.

Tell us which one sounds like you and we'll take it from there. Nobody needs prior experience with sensory aids, with 3D printing, or with nonprofits.

Students

We're looking for motivated students to join our team to help with designing and printing sensory aids, running distributions, and bringing Venturable to your own school.

Educators & therapists

If you work with kids who would benefit from sensory aids, request Venturable's services for your classroom, practice, or center. We'll bring the tools to you.

Parents & families

Looking for support for your child? Reach out. There's no cost, no diagnosis requirement, and no application to fill out beyond telling us what would help.

What we need right now

  • Motivated students to join the team.
  • Funding for future projects — printers, filament, and event costs.
  • Organizations to partner with — schools, therapy offices, pediatricians, and community centers across Ventura County.

What we ask of volunteers

A few hours a month and a willingness to finish what you start. That's the whole commitment. If it stops fitting your schedule, just tell us. It's completely fine.

Donate

Funding turns directly into tools.

We're youth-led with no salaries and no office. Donations go into the things that actually produce sensory aids: printers, filament, and the cost of running distribution events across the county.

Where your money goes

$10 A spool of filament — enough for a couple dozen printed fidgets.
$25 A set of sensory aids for a whole classroom.
$60 Supplies to run one distribution event.
$150 A 3D printer to start a program at a new school.

Other ways to give

  • In-kind donations — filament, printers, or supplies we'd otherwise buy.
  • Employer matching — many companies double a donation; ask your HR team.
  • Sponsor a school — fund a 3D printing program at one site and we'll credit you on its page.
