Free sensory tools for kids who need them.

We're Venturable, a student-run group in Ventura County. We make and give out free fidget toys and sensory aids to neurodivergent kids, because we don't think cost should decide whether a kid gets support.

Get involved What we do
Our mission

Every child should be able to access emotional support.

Venturable is a youth-led organization that provides free fidget toys and sensory aids to neurodivergent and neurodevelopmentally diverse children across Ventura County, making sure that cost is not a barrier to supportive systems. We believe that every child, regardless of socioeconomic status, should be able to access emotional support to further their well-being.

We print

We provide 3D printing resources to middle and high school students, turning a school lab into a source of well-made support tools.

We distribute

We give fidget toys and sensory aids to children with Autism, ADHD, or anyone facing difficult situations in their lives for free.

We show up

We partner with schools, therapy offices, pediatricians, and community centers to host events and distributions. Our goal is to reach kids wherever they are.

Why it matters

These are essential devices, not extras.

Sensory aids aren't luxuries. They are essential devices for many children, especially neurodiverse ones. Currently, these tools can quickly add up in cost and they don't represent what young people actually want from their support systems.

We think being students ourselves is a big part of why this works. We're close in age to the kids we're making things for, so we have a pretty good sense of what they'll actually use versus what just ends up in a drawer.

Every contribution makes a difference to a child in need.

We're looking for motivated students to join our team, funding for future projects, and organizations to partner with.
