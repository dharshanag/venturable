Venturable
About us

We're two students trying to make this easier.

Venturable is a student-run group in Ventura County. We get free sensory tools to the kids who need them — at no cost, and without making it a big deal.

Mission

What we're here to do

Venturable is a youth-led organization that provides free fidget toys and sensory aids to neurodivergent and neurodevelopmentally diverse children across Ventura County, making sure that cost is not a barrier to supportive systems.

We believe that every child, regardless of socioeconomic status, should be able to access emotional support to further their well-being.

Vision

Where we're headed

Venturable aims to meet the emotional needs of youth in Ventura County by breaking the stigma of mental health support.

We aim to equip students with the tools necessary to thrive at home, in school, and in their community.

Why it matters

Sensory aids aren't luxuries.

They are essential devices for many children, especially neurodiverse ones. Currently, these tools can quickly add up in cost, and they don't represent what young people actually want from their support systems.

We're students ourselves, so we know what other kids actually want to use. So that's what we try to make, and we hand it out for free.

Who we serve

We go where the kids already are.

Venturable partners with local organizations to host community engagement events and device distribution services across Ventura County.

Schools

Where students spend most of their week and where a sensory tool does the most good during the school day.

Therapy offices

Working alongside the professionals who already know each child's needs, so what we provide actually fits.

Pediatricians

Reaching families at the point where a diagnosis or a concern first comes up, before cost becomes the obstacle.

Community centers

Meeting kids outside of school and clinical settings, in the places their families already trust.

Meet the team

The students behind Venturable

Both founders are current high school sophomores in Ventura County.

AM

Aarush Mane

Co-founder & Co-President

Sophomore at Oak Park High School. Aarush runs the making side of Venturable: the 3D printing program, the designs we produce, and getting finished sensory aids out to the kids waiting for them.

DG

Dharshana Gopinath

Co-founder & Co-President

Sophomore at Agoura High School. Dharshana leads outreach and partnerships: connecting Venturable with the schools, therapy offices, pediatricians, and community centers that bring us to families across the county.
