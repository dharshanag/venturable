Projects

What we're building and where it goes.

Three programs, one goal: get sensory tools into the hands of Ventura County kids without a price tag attached.

Core program Schools

3D printing resources

We provide 3D printing resources to middle and high school students, so the tools get made close to the kids who need them. Students learn to design and produce real sensory aids, and the output goes straight into distribution.

It's the part of Venturable that scales: one school lab can supply a whole community, and the students running it build a skill along the way.

Core program Distribution

Fidget toys & sensory aids

We distribute fidget toys and sensory aids to children with Autism, ADHD, or anyone facing difficult situations in their lives. There is no cost, no eligibility form, and no requirement to have a diagnosis on file.

Cost is the whole thing we're trying to remove, so we're not going to pile on a bunch of paperwork in its place.

Core program Community

Community engagement events

We partner with local schools, therapy offices, pediatricians, and community centers to host engagement events and device distribution services across Ventura County.

Our goal is to reach kids wherever they are — which means going to the places families already trust rather than asking them to come find us.
